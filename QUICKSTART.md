# Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 Get Started in 3 Steps

1. **Install & Run**
```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

2. **Open Browser**
   - Go to [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)

3. **Start Recording**
   - Click "Start Recording"
   - Select your screen
   - Click "Stop Recording" when done

## 📹 Main Features

### Record Screen
- **Click** "Start Recording"
- **Select** screen/window to share
- **Allow** microphone (optional)
- **Click** "Stop Recording"
- **Preview** your video

### Trim Video
- **Click** "Trim Video" after recording
- **Adjust** start/end time sliders
- **Click** "Trim Video" button
- **Download** or share trimmed result

### Upload & Share
- **Click** "Upload & Share"
- **Wait** for upload to complete
- **Copy** the share link
- **Share** with anyone

### View Analytics
- **Open** any shared video link
- **See** view count and watch percentage
- **Track** engagement metrics

## 💡 Tips

- Use Chrome or Edge for best results
- Grant microphone permission for audio
- Videos save as .webm format
- All data stored locally

## 📊 What Gets Tracked

When someone watches your shared video:
- ✅ View count increments when they click play
- ✅ Watch percentage tracked at 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%
- ✅ Average completion calculated automatically

## 🎯 Quick Test Flow

1. Record a short video (10-15 seconds)
2. Trim it if desired
3. Upload and get share link
4. Open link and play video
5. Check analytics below the video

Enjoy your Screen Recorder! 🎥
